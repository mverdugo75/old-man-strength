Same three muscle groups every week — the main lift rotates every other week so each one gets a real focused week to build strength.
Staying Sharp
Log every session. Weight and reps, even in the phone Notes app — six main lifts rotating in and out means a log is what makes the progress visible.

Rotate a small accessory every 3-4 weeks. Bench, OHP, Row, Pulldown, Squat, and Deadlift already give plenty of variety on their own.

2-3 Reps in Reserve always. No chasing failure — consistency over years beats intensity for a few weeks.
